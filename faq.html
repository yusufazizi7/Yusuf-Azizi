E-book Help

E-book Questions

Common questions about purchasing and using Islamic Qalam digital e-books.

I need to download my e-book again. What should I do?

If you purchased while signed in to Islamic Qalam, open your profile and go to Purchased Items. You can generate a new temporary download link whenever you need your e-book again.

If you purchased as a guest, visit the Retrieve Download Link page and enter the email address you used when purchasing. A new download link will be sent to that email address.

Why has my download link expired?

Islamic Qalam download links are temporary for security and are valid for 15 minutes. This does not mean your purchase has expired.

Your purchase remains available permanently. You can generate or request another download link whenever you need one.

Can I share my e-book with family or friends?

Yes. You may privately share your purchased Islamic Qalam e-book with family members or friends.

However, the e-book should not be uploaded, published or distributed publicly or on a large scale. This includes uploading the complete e-book to public websites, social media pages, large public groups, file-sharing platforms or similar services.

Private sharing with family and friends is permitted. Public or mass distribution is not permitted.
Does my e-book purchase expire?

No. Once your payment has been successfully completed, your purchase does not expire.

The individual download links are temporary, but you can request a new one whenever you need to download the e-book again.

Do I need an Islamic Qalam account to purchase an e-book?

No. You can purchase an e-book as a guest without creating an Islamic Qalam account.

If you purchase while signed in, your e-books will also appear under Purchased Items in your profile, making it easier to access them again later.

I didn't receive my download email. What should I do?

First, check your spam or junk folder and make sure you are checking the same email address that was used during checkout.

If you purchased as a guest, you can also use the Retrieve Download Link page to request another email.

What if I no longer have access to the email I purchased with?

Contact Islamic Qalam and provide enough information for us to verify the original purchase.

Once the purchase can be verified, we can help you recover access to your e-book.

What format are Islamic Qalam e-books provided in?

Islamic Qalam e-books are digital products that can be downloaded and kept on your device.

The available format will be shown on the e-book's product page before purchase.

Can I print my purchased e-book?

Yes. You may print a copy of your purchased e-book for your own personal use.

Can I resell or publicly distribute the e-book?

No. Purchasing an e-book does not give permission to resell the Islamic Qalam edition or distribute it publicly on a large scale.

Islamic Qalam permits reasonable private sharing with family and friends, but public, commercial or mass distribution is not permitted.
